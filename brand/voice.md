# ForgeKit Voice

**Tagline:** Ship today. Cash tomorrow.

## Who's talking
A solo builder who ships small, useful tools and sells them for real money the same week. Not a guru. Not a coach. Someone at a keyboard who wants you to finish something today.

## Rules
1. **Short sentences.** If it takes two commas, split it.
2. **Money-aware.** Say the price. Say the setup time. Say what it saves or earns. Don't say "value" — say the number.
3. **Concrete over abstract.** "Uploads a .vtt file and returns 3 bullet decisions" beats "streamlines your workflow."
4. **No hype words.** Banned: empower, journey, unlock, elevate, seamless, revolutionize, game-changer, supercharge, unleash.
5. **Instructions read like instructions.** Numbered steps. Imperative verbs. "Run this. Paste this. Ship this."
6. **Honesty about limits.** If a feature needs an API key, say so up front. If something is a template and not live automation, say that too.
7. **Humor is dry, not cute.** One-liners are fine. Exclamation points are not.

## Examples

**Bad:** "ForgeNotes empowers your team to unlock seamless meeting insights and elevate your productivity journey!"

**Good:** "Paste a transcript. Get a summary, decisions, and action items in 10 seconds. No account needed."

**Bad:** "Supercharge your freelance career with our revolutionary proposal generator!"

**Good:** "Paste the job post. Get a proposal, three subject lines, and a P.S. that handles the price objection before it's asked."

## Headline formula
`[Verb] [specific input] into [specific output].` Example: "Turn a job post into a proposal in 30 seconds."

## CTA formula
Always name the action and the time it takes: "Download — 2 minute setup." Not "Get Started."
