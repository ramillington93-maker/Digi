# ForgeResume — 20-second demo recording steps

Record at 1280x720 or larger. Have the terminal and browser ready before
you hit record — no dead air.

1. Start the app before recording: `streamlit run app.py`, wait for it to
   open in the browser, and clear any leftover input from a prior run.
2. Start screen recording.
3. On the left panel, click "Upload .txt" under "1. Your resume" and
   select `samples/resume_1.txt` (or paste its text — either works, but
   upload is faster on camera).
4. On the right panel, click "Upload .txt" under "2. Target job
   description" and select `samples/jd_1.txt`.
5. Click **Analyze match**.
6. Hold on the match score metric (25.0%) and the progress bar for 2
   seconds.
7. Scroll down slightly to show the matched-keywords chips and the
   missing-keywords chips side by side.
8. Paste this into the "Weak bullet" box: `Helped with social media
   posting schedule across Instagram and Facebook.`
9. Click **Rewrite bullet** and hold on the STAR-format output for 2-3
   seconds.
10. Stop recording.
11. Trim dead frames at the start/end so the final clip lands at ~18-20
    seconds.

No voiceover needed — the on-screen score, chips, and rewrite carry the
demo on their own.
