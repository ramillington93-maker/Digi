Job Title: Marketing Manager, Growth
Company: Fernbridge Consumer Goods

About the role:
We're hiring a Marketing Manager to own growth campaigns across email, paid
social, and SEO for our direct-to-consumer brand. You'll manage the
marketing budget, run A/B tests, and report results to leadership monthly.

Responsibilities:
- Own the email marketing program end to end, including segmentation and
  automation workflows.
- Manage paid social campaigns across Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok with
  a defined budget and target ROAS.
- Run A/B tests on landing pages and email subject lines, analyze results,
  and iterate.
- Build and maintain a content calendar across channels.
- Use Google Analytics and HubSpot to track conversion rate, retention,
  and customer acquisition cost.
- Collaborate with design and product teams on campaign assets.
- Manage vendor relationships and negotiate contracts.
- Present monthly performance reports with clear KPIs to leadership.
- Optimize SEO for the marketing blog and product pages.
- Lead a small team of two marketing coordinators.

Requirements:
- 4+ years of marketing experience, ideally in a growth or performance role.
- Hands-on experience with HubSpot, Google Analytics, and A/B testing tools.
- Strong budget management and ROI reporting skills.
- Experience with SEO, paid social, and email automation.
- Proven track record of increasing conversion rate and retention.
- Comfortable presenting data-driven results to leadership.
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, or related field.

Nice to have:
- Experience managing a marketing team.
- Familiarity with TikTok advertising.
- Copywriting skills for landing pages and email.
