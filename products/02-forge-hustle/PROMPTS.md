# PROMPTS.md — 15 prompts for running an AI side hustle

Copy, paste into ChatGPT or Claude, replace the text in `[brackets]`. Same 15 prompts live in `databases/Prompts.csv` if you'd rather import them into Notion.

## Offer testing

**1. Pain Point Digger**
> I want to sell [service] to [target customer]. List 10 specific pain points this audience complains about online, phrased in their own words.

**2. Offer Sharpener**
> Turn this rough idea into a one-sentence offer using the format "I help [who] do [what] without [pain], in [timeframe]": [rough idea]

## Pricing

**3. Price Anchor Finder**
> I'm pricing [service] for [target customer]. List 3 comparable services or tools they already pay for, with rough prices, so I can anchor my price against them.

**4. Tier Builder**
> Turn this single offer into a 3-tier pricing table (Basic/Standard/Premium) with one clear reason to upgrade at each tier: [offer description]

## Launch

**5. Launch Tweet Thread**
> Write a 5-tweet build-in-public thread announcing [product]. Tweet 1 is a hook about the problem, tweets 2-4 show progress or proof, tweet 5 has one clear CTA and link.

**6. Cold DM Opener**
> Write 3 short cold DMs (under 40 words each) pitching [offer] to [target customer]. No hype words. End each with one specific question.

## Gumroad

**7. Gumroad Blurb**
> Write Gumroad sales copy for [product]: a title under 60 characters, a subtitle under 90 characters, a 100-word pitch, and 5 bullet points focused on outcomes, not features.

**8. FAQ Generator**
> List the 6 most likely buyer objections for [product] priced at $[price], then answer each in 2 sentences or less, in a plain, non-salesy tone.

## Content

**9. Hook Generator**
> Write 8 short-form hooks (under 12 words each) about [topic] for [platform]. Mix curiosity, numbers, and contrarian angles. No emojis.

**10. Proof Post**
> Turn this result into a LinkedIn post: [result/metric]. Start with the number, explain what I did in 3 short sentences, end with a soft CTA.

## Experiment design

**11. Experiment Framer**
> Turn this idea into a 7-day experiment: [idea]. Give me a hypothesis, one metric to track, and a clear pass/fail bar before I start.

**12. Kill Criteria**
> I've run [experiment] for [X] days and gotten [result]. Should I keep going, pivot, or kill it? Give me a blunt answer with the reasoning in 3 bullets.

## Client delivery

**13. Scope Tightener**
> Turn this client request into a tight scope with 3 deliverables, 1 revision round, and a deadline: [client request]

**14. Objection Handler**
> A client said: "[objection]". Write 2 short, non-defensive replies that address the concern and move the conversation toward a yes.

## Weekly review

**15. Weekly Numbers Summary**
> Here's this week's revenue and content data: [paste rows]. Summarize in 4 sentences: what worked, what didn't, and one specific action for next week.
