## FAQ

**Is this a Notion template link or files I download?**
You get the CSVs and Markdown files. You import them into your own Notion workspace — you own the resulting database, not a shared template link.

**Do I need Notion's paid plan?**
No, the free plan handles this fine.

**How long does setup actually take?**
15-20 minutes, most of it in the one manual relation-linking step (explained exactly in IMPORT.md).

**Can I delete the sample data?**
Yes — it's there so you see the structure working, then you replace it with your own.

**Does it track revenue automatically?**
No. You log revenue rows yourself. This is a tracker, not a payment integration.
