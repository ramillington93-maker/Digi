# How to use this system

ForgeHustle is five linked databases and a weekly loop. That's it. No dashboards to configure, no automations to wire up.

## The five databases

- **Offers** — every idea you're selling or considering. One row per offer.
- **Experiments** — every test you run to validate an offer (a post, a DM batch, a price test).
- **Content** — every piece of content you publish, linked to the offer it supports.
- **Revenue** — every dollar that comes in, linked to the offer that earned it.
- **Prompts** — reusable AI prompts, organized by category, so you're not rewriting the same prompt every week.

Offers is the hub. Experiments, Content, and Revenue all point back to it. That's the whole system.

## The weekly loop

1. **Monday** — Open Offers. Sort by Status. Pick one offer to move forward this week (Idea → Testing → Validated, or straight to Killed if it's dead).
2. **Through the week** — Log every experiment you run and every piece of content you publish, linked to that offer. Log revenue the day it lands, not at month-end. You will not remember the exact number in three weeks.
3. **Friday** — Fill out the Weekly Review (see "Weekly review template.md"). Look at what actually made money, not what felt productive.

## Rules that keep this useful

1. **One offer, one row.** Don't create a new offer row for a small variation. Note the variation and move on.
2. **Kill offers on purpose.** A Status of "Killed" is not a failure log — it's how you stop wasting Monday mornings on dead ideas. Set a Next Action of "None" when you kill something.
3. **Every experiment gets a result.** Win, Loss, Partial, or Testing. "I'll know later" is not a result — set an End Date and come back to it.
4. **Revenue rows are facts, not projections.** Log money that landed. A signed contract is not revenue until it's paid.
5. **Prompts are a starting point.** Copy one, paste it, replace the brackets. If you rewrite the same prompt three times, save your version back into the Prompts database.

## What this system will not do

It will not tell you which offer to build. It will not write your content for you (the Prompts database gets you most of the way there — see "Weekly review template.md" and PROMPTS.md). It's a tracker, not an oracle. It works if you fill it in honestly and look at it once a week.
