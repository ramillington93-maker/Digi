## Tip on inputs

Short, phrase-length offer and ICP fields read more naturally than full
sentences — "a Notion template for freelance designers" beats "I made a
Notion template that helps freelance designers track their invoices."
The generator works either way, but the shorter the input, the tighter
the email.
