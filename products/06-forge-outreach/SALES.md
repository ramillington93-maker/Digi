# ForgeOutreach — Gumroad listing copy

## Title
ForgeOutreach

## Subtitle
Turn an offer, an ICP, and a proof point into a 5-email cold outreach sequence.

## Pitch (~120 words)

Cold email is a first-draft problem. You know your offer, you know who
you're selling to, and you still burn an hour staring at a blank
subject line. ForgeOutreach fixes that part. Type in your offer, your
ideal customer, and one proof point — a stat, a testimonial, a result —
and it builds a full 5-email sequence: intro on day 0, value/proof on
day 2, case study on day 4, objection handling on day 7, and a breakup
email on day 12. Every email ships with two subject lines for A/B
testing and a matching LinkedIn DM. Export a `.txt` for reading or a
`.csv` shaped for Instantly, Lemlist, or Smartlead. No API key needed.
Runs local, one command, private.

## 5 bullets

- 5-email sequence (day 0, 2, 4, 7, 12) built from your offer, ICP, and one proof point — real templating logic, not a fill-in-the-blank Google Doc.
- Two subject lines (A and B) per email, plus a LinkedIn DM variant for every day in the sequence.
- One-click `.csv` export shaped for Instantly / Lemlist / Smartlead bulk upload, plus a plain `.txt` export.
- No API key required for the full sequence. Optional AI polish if you add your own Anthropic or OpenAI key.
- Runs local with one command (`streamlit run app.py`) — your offer and customer data never leave your machine.

## Who it's for

- Freelancers and agencies who need outreach sequences for their own
  pipeline or a client's, fast.
- Solo founders doing their own cold outreach before hiring an SDR.
- Anyone running Instantly, Lemlist, Smartlead, or Apollo who needs
  sequence content, not another sending tool.

## Who it's not for

- Anyone looking for a tool that sends emails. This only generates
  text — you still need your own sequencer or email client to send.
- Anyone who wants fully custom, hand-written copy for a single
  high-stakes prospect. This is a sequence builder, not a copywriter
  for one email.
- Teams that need a shared, hosted, multi-user tool. This runs local,
  one person at a time.

## FAQ

**Does this actually send the emails?**
No. It generates the sequence as text. You send it through your own
email client or sequencing tool.

**Do I need to pay for an API key?**
No. The full 5-email sequence works with zero API keys. An optional AI
polish pass is available if you add your own Anthropic or OpenAI key —
entirely your choice, entirely your cost.

**What format is the export?**
A `.txt` file for reading, and a `.csv` with columns `day, subject_a,
subject_b, body, linkedin_dm` — built to paste straight into Instantly,
Lemlist, or Smartlead's bulk upload.

**Can I reuse this for multiple clients?**
Yes. One purchase, unlimited personal or client projects. See
`LICENSE.txt`.

**What do I need to run it?**
Python 3.11 and one terminal command: `streamlit run app.py`.
