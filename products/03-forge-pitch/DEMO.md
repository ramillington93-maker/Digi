# Recording a 20-second demo

Exact steps to record a short screen-capture demo of ForgePitch for the
Gumroad listing.

1. Open a terminal in the `03-forge-pitch` folder. Start screen
   recording (0:00).
2. Run: `streamlit run app.py` and wait for the browser tab to open.
   (0:00–0:03)
3. In the left column, open the "Load a sample job post" dropdown and
   pick `job_post_1.txt`. (0:03–0:05)
4. Check the "Use sample profile" box in the right column so the
   profile fills in. (0:05–0:06)
5. Click "Generate proposal." (0:06–0:07)
6. Let the page scroll into view: subject lines, proposal body, price
   anchors, and the P.S. line. Scroll slowly down the result once.
   (0:07–0:16)
7. Click "Download as Markdown" to show the export button working.
   (0:16–0:18)
8. Stop recording. (0:18–0:20)
