## FAQ

**Do I need an API key or subscription?**
No. The CSV and hook bank are the whole product and need nothing installed. The remixer tool is a bonus and works without a key too.

**What niche is the calendar written for?**
"AI tools" is the demo niche in the CSV, chosen because the hook pattern is easy to see and swap. `HOOKS.md` includes 3 more niches as direct examples.

**Can I use this for client work?**
Yes. The license covers unlimited personal and client projects.

**Is the remixer an automation tool?**
No. It rewrites hooks you paste in. You still post everything yourself.

**What if I want AI-generated rewrites instead of rule-based?**
Add your own `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` or `OPENAI_API_KEY` to a `.env` file and the remixer upgrades automatically. Fully optional.
