# 50 Hooks by Niche

Swap the subject, keep the structure. These are written for AI tools, freelance,
fitness coaching, and local services — but the sentence shapes work for any niche.
Use `calendar-30-day.csv` to slot them into a real posting schedule, or paste one
into the hook remixer (`app.py`) for 5 variants in a different structure.
