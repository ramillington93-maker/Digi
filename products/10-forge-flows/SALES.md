4 n8n automations that log sales, summarize transcripts, and send you a daily task digest.

## Pitch (~120 words)

You're doing the same three tasks by hand every day: logging sales, writing up meeting notes, and checking what's due. ForgeFlows replaces them with four n8n workflows you import once and forget.

Sell something on Gumroad, it logs itself to a sheet and pings your Slack. Drop a transcript in a folder, get a summary markdown file. Bookmark something worth writing about, it lands in your content-ideas sheet. Every morning, your open tasks show up in Slack and your inbox before you've opened your laptop.

Import the JSON, add your own credentials, hit Active. No code to write. Zapier recipes included if you don't run n8n. Setup: 10-15 minutes per workflow.

## 5 bullets

- 4 ready-to-import n8n workflow files — Gumroad sale logging, transcript summarizing, bookmark capture, daily task digest
- Matching Zapier recipes for all 4 flows, if you'd rather not run n8n
- Dummy test payloads for every trigger, so you can test before connecting a live source
- FLOWS.md tells you exactly which field to change in every single node, before you run anything
- No code required — import, add credentials, activate

## Who it's for

Solo builders and small teams running a product or service business who already use (or are willing to set up) n8n or Zapier, Google Sheets, and Slack. Good fit if you're tired of manually copying sale data into a spreadsheet or writing your own meeting summaries.

## Who it's not for

Not for you if you want a finished, hosted app with no setup — this is a template pack that needs your own n8n or Zapier account and 10-15 minutes of credential setup per workflow. Not for you if you need a live AI summarizer built in — the transcript workflow's summarization step is a stub you point at your own script or AI API.

## FAQ

**Do I need n8n or Zapier already set up?**
You need an account on one of them. n8n is free to self-host; n8n Cloud and Zapier both have free tiers that may cover light use.

**Does this include a summarizer for the transcript workflow?**
No. That node is a stub — point it at your own script or an AI API of your choice. Said plainly in the README.

**Will I get updates if n8n changes its node format?**
This is a one-time digital download, sold as-is per the license. No update subscription included.

**Refunds?**
Handled through your Gumroad purchase page.
