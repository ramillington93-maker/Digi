### r/EntrepreneurRideAlong

**Title:** Shipped 10 products in one go instead of iterating on 1 — here's why

Common advice is "pick one product, go deep." I did the opposite this round: built 10 small tools, each solving one specific problem a freelancer or solo operator has this week (writing a proposal, summarizing a meeting, filling a content calendar, tracking a side hustle in Notion).

Why: a $19–$25 tool that solves one problem in under a minute is an easier yes than a $99 tool that asks someone to change their whole workflow. Ten small yeses beat chasing one big yes.

Every product ships with a sample output so you can see the value before running anything — that was the single highest-leverage thing I did for conversion in early testing on the listing pages themselves.

Curious if anyone else here has run the "many small products vs. one big product" experiment and what you saw.
